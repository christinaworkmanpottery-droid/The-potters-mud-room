Why Pottery Pricing Is Hard

Most potters struggle with pricing because pottery has hidden costs that are easy to forget:

  • Materials aren't just clay and glaze — underglazes, decals, trimming tools, packaging all add up
  • Firing costs money — electricity, kiln wear, and often two firings per piece
  • Studio overhead gets forgotten — rent, utilities, website, market fees
  • Your time has value — throwing, trimming, decorating, glazing, finishing
  • Breakage happens — successful pieces need to cover casualties

Without tracking all these costs, you might be losing money on every piece you sell.

What The Pricing Calculator Does

  • Divide your monthly studio costs across the number of pieces you make
  • Apply standard craft markup (2×, 2.5×, 3×, or custom)
  • See a complete price breakdown so you understand where your money goes
  • Save calculations and compare pricing across different pieces

Example: Pricing a Celadon Bowl

Inputs:

  • • Clay: $2.50
  • • Glaze (celadon): $1.25
  • • Firing (bisque + glaze): $3.00
  • • Labor (2 hours × $25/hr): $50.00
  • • Monthly studio rent: $400 ÷ 40 pieces = $10.00/piece
  • • Other monthly costs: $100 ÷ 40 pieces = $2.50/piece

Calculation:

Total Cost = $2.50 + $1.25 + $3.00 + $50.00 + $10.00 + $2.50 = $69.25

Suggested Price (2.5× markup) = $69.25 × 2.5 = $173.13

The calculator shows you exactly where your costs come from, so you can price confidently and make a sustainable living from your pottery.

How It Works

  1. Enter per-piece costs: clay, glaze, supplies, firing
  2. Add monthly studio costs: rent, utilities, overhead
  3. Tell us your production: how many pieces you make per month
  4. Set your hourly rate: what you want to pay yourself
  5. Enter time spent: hours for this specific piece
  6. Choose your markup: 2×, 2.5×, 3×, or custom
  7. See your suggested price: complete breakdown + profit margin

The calculator automatically divides your monthly costs across your pieces, so one bowl doesn't get charged your full monthly rent. It's smarter than manual calculation and faster than spreadsheets.
